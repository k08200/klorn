Questions

Do I have to connect every account separately?

Yes, once each. Signing in brings your first Google account; add the others from Settings, and from then on they all arrive in the same list.

Does Klorn send or delete mail on its own?

No. Lanes only sort. Replies, deletes and archives wait for your approval. If you turn on automation, the assistant may run low-risk steps by itself, and sending is never one of them.

Which accounts work today?

Gmail and Google Calendar, plus Naver Mail and iCloud Mail in beta. That’s the full list today; this page changes the day another account can be connected.

Where does my mail go?

To Klorn’s classifier and back. Tokens are encrypted at rest, and the code that touches them is public. If you’d rather hold it yourself, run Klorn on your own server.

What does “bring your own AI key” mean?

Klorn can use your own key from a model provider, or a local model, instead of ours. It works on any plan.
