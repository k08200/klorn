The fine print, plainly

Why there is a cap at all

Sorting mail into lanes, with the reason on each message, costs little to run, so it stays free. Drafting replies is the part with a real AI bill behind it, so that is the part in Pro. A per-day AI budget is what lets sorting stay free without an open-ended bill: when the cap is reached, Klorn stops spending rather than overspending, and says so.

Bring your own key

Your own model key works on Pro: point Klorn at it instead of ours. Self-hosted installs can use any key or a local model, and run the same classifier with the same limits; there is no second-class self-host path.

Cancelling

Cancel anytime, in the app. Refund terms are in the refund policy. Prices are in USD.
