and the current `main`. Each row: verdict, where the control lives (file:line), and the
comment to paste into the TAC portal. Line numbers reflect `main` as of 2026-08-13.

exist in this architecture (stated evidence proves absence). NOTE: compliant with a
remark an assessor may probe.
